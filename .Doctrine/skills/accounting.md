# Skill: Accounting

> Load with `roles/finance-ops.md`. The bookkeeping/accounting discipline —
> statements, journals, reconciliation, the close. Distinct from `skills/finance.md`
> (runway, budgets, cost models, unit economics — the forward-looking view): this is
> *the books*. **Draft-and-analyze only — a qualified accountant/CPA reviews before
> anything is filed or relied on; Finance Ops never moves money (§5).**

## Principles
- **Double-entry or it doesn't balance.** Every transaction hits at least two
  accounts; debits equal credits, always. A trial balance that doesn't zero is a
  defect, not a rounding quirk.
- **The books reconcile to reality.** Recorded balances tie to the bank/processor
  statements. An unreconciled difference is unfinished work, never "close enough".
- **One source of truth per number.** "Revenue", "cash", "MRR" are each defined
  once (with `skills/data-analytics.md`'s one-metric-one-definition) — the
  statements and the dashboard must agree or one is wrong.
- **Cutoff is sacred.** A transaction lands in the period it economically occurred
  (accrual), not when cash moved — unless you've explicitly chosen cash-basis and said so.
- **Auditable by a stranger.** Every entry carries its date, accounts, amount, and a
  reference to source evidence. If an auditor can't trace it, it isn't done.

## The core work
- **Financial statements** — the three that tie together: income statement (P&L),
  balance sheet (assets = liabilities + equity), cash-flow statement. Net income
  flows to retained earnings; cash-flow ties to the balance-sheet cash delta — or
  one of them is wrong.
- **Journal entries** — drafted with a clear narrative and source reference;
  accruals, prepaids, depreciation, and corrections are *entries*, never edits to history.
- **Reconciliation** — tie each account (bank, card, clearing, AR/AP) to its
  external statement; list and explain every difference; nothing written off silently.
- **Month/period-end close** — a repeatable checklist: accrue, reconcile, review
  variances, lock the period. The close is *done* when it's reproducible, not heroic.
- **Variance analysis** — budget vs. actual explained in plain language (price,
  volume, timing, one-offs), so a non-accountant can act on it.
- **Audit support** — assemble the schedules, tie-outs, and evidence an auditor
  asks for; a clean workpaper trail is the deliverable.

## The guardrail (non-negotiable for this domain)
- **Draft + analyze, never file or pay.** Statements, entries, and filings are
  prepared for a qualified professional to review and sign; moving money, filing
  taxes, and finalizing statutory accounts are the human's (§5). Label estimates as
  estimates; never present a draft as filed.
- **Never fabricate a number.** A figure with no source is a placeholder, not a
  balance (§1). "Unknown" is a valid cell; an invented one is a defect.
- **Financial/personal data is regulated** — apply `skills/privacy-compliance.md`
  (retention, access, PII in exported workpapers).

## Review checklist
- [ ] Trial balance zeroes; the three statements tie to each other
- [ ] Every account reconciled to its external statement; differences explained
- [ ] Each entry has date, accounts, amount, narrative, and a source reference
- [ ] Cutoff and basis (accrual vs cash) stated and applied consistently
- [ ] Metric definitions match the analytics/reporting definitions
- [ ] Draft labelled for professional review; no filing or payment performed

## Common failure modes
A P&L that doesn't reconcile to the balance sheet; "reconciled" with an unexplained
plug; cash-basis and accrual mixed within one period; a fabricated figure filling a
gap the source didn't cover; a heroic close nobody can reproduce next month; PII
sitting unredacted in an exported workpaper.
